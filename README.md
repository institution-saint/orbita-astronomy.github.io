# ORBITA --- Astronomy & Space

> **Look farther. Discover more.**

ORBITA is a modern astronomy storefront built for people who look up at
the night sky and want to understand what they are seeing.

The concept combines astronomy equipment, observation accessories, star
maps, educational resources, and space-inspired objects into an
experience that feels closer to a small digital observatory than a
conventional ecommerce catalog.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🌌 The ORBITA Concept

ORBITA is organized around the experience of observing the universe.

The store is designed for:

-   First-time stargazers
-   Amateur astronomers
-   Backyard observers
-   Families discovering astronomy
-   Space enthusiasts
-   Experienced observers looking for useful accessories

The goal is not simply to sell a telescope.

**The goal is to make the night sky easier to explore.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🔭 Explore the Sky

The storefront can guide visitors through different observation
experiences.

### BEGIN

For people buying their first astronomy equipment.

### OBSERVE

For telescopes, binoculars, eyepieces, filters and observation
accessories.

### NAVIGATE

For star charts, planispheres, red-light tools and celestial navigation
resources.

### DISCOVER

For educational astronomy products, books, models and space exploration
content.

### DEEP SKY

For more advanced observation equipment and accessories.

electronics stores.

-   Cosmic Violet --- `#413B72`
-   Starlight White --- `#F4F6FA`

### Design language

-   Dark cinematic backgrounds
-   Starfield photography
-   Metallic details
-   Thin orbital lines
-   Large astronomical imagery
-   Gold accents

-   High-contrast typography

## 📸 Photography Direction

ORBITA imagery should be realistic, clean and immersive.

Preferred photography:

-   Realistic telescopes
-   Astronomy binoculars
-   Star maps
-   Observation accessories
-   Night landscapes
-   Clear starfields
-   Moon and planetary imagery
-   Observatory environments
-   Telescope setups under real night skies

Product photographs should:

-   Show the complete product
-   Keep the subject properly centered
-   Avoid unnecessary cropping
-   Use clean backgrounds when appropriate
-   Maintain consistent proportions
-   Avoid embedded website text
-   Avoid prices or buttons
-   Avoid unrelated products
-   Avoid watermarks and UI screenshots

Each catalog product should have its own dedicated local image.

## 🚀 GitHub Pages

ORBITA is structured for static GitHub Pages hosting.

After uploading the project:

1.  Open the repository.
2.  Go to **Settings**.
3.  Open **Pages**.
4.  Select the deployment source.
5.  Choose the main branch.
6.  Save the configuration.
7.  Wait for GitHub Pages to publish the site.

Relative paths and local assets are used so the project remains portable
between local development and GitHub Pages.
